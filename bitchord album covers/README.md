# BitChord Album Covers

Place custom album covers (images or videos) here.

When custom covers are uploaded or dropped via BitChord Web's "Custom Album Cover" menu:
- Images are automatically cropped and centered 1:1 to prevent stretching.
- Supports videos (MP4, WebM) and images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG).
- Custom covers are bound to specific songs by track or video ID, or set globally.
